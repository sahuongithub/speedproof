What did not work

Most real optimisations turn out not to be measurable, and the reasons are more interesting than the successes. Nobody appears to have published what fraction of real optimisation work can be measured at all, so here it is.

Of 44 candidate optimisations mined from four projects' histories, 1 could be measured cleanly and showed the improvement its author intended.

The dominant reason is not the pipeline. It is that a project's benchmark suite is written to track overall health, not to cover the specific places where optimisation work happens. A project can have benchmarks, and have optimisation commits, and have the two never meet.
